Policy Overview

Return Policy

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to return any item for a full refund or free size exchange. After 30 days, we can only offer an exchange or store credit.

All items must be returned in their original unworn condition — with tags attached, unwashed, and in the original packaging where possible.

✓ Eligible for Return
  • Unworn, unwashed items
  • Items with original tags attached
  • Returned within 30 days
  • Original packaging preferred
  • Wrong size received
  • Faulty or defective items
✕ Not Eligible
  • Worn or washed items
  • Items without tags
  • After 30 days of delivery
  • Items marked Final Sale
  • Accessories (caps, bags)
  • Customised or altered items
Return Process

How to Return

Starting a return takes less than 2 minutes. Here's the process:

1
Email Us

Send an email to returns@expectations.co with your order number and reason for return. We'll respond within 24 hours.

2
Receive Return Label

We'll email you a prepaid return shipping label. Print it and attach to your package. Return shipping is free for size exchanges and faulty items.

3
Pack & Drop Off

Pack the item securely, attach the label, and drop it at any DHL or FedEx drop-off point. Keep your receipt as proof of postage.

4
Refund or Exchange

Once we receive and inspect your return (usually 2–3 days), your refund or exchange will be processed immediately.

Size Exchanges

Exchanges

Size exchanges are always free. Simply follow the return process above and note your desired size in the email. We'll ship the new size as soon as we receive your return.

If your desired size is out of stock, we'll offer a full refund or store credit.

Refunds

Refunds

Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of us receiving your return. You'll receive an email confirmation when your refund is initiated.

Stripe processes refunds within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. If you haven't received your refund after 10 days, contact your bank first, then email us.

Note: Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a faulty item.

Faulty Items

Faulty or Wrong Items

If you received a faulty item or the wrong item, we sincerely apologise. Email us at returns@expectations.co with:

— Your order number
— A photo of the fault or wrong item
— Whether you'd prefer a replacement or refund

We'll cover all return shipping costs and prioritise your replacement or refund. Response within 24 hours guaranteed.
