All EXPECTATIONS pieces are designed with an intentional oversized silhouette. When in doubt, size down — our cuts are generous by design.

How To Measure
Get Your
Exact Fit
01
Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal under your arms.

02
Shoulders

Measure across your upper back from shoulder point to shoulder point.

03
Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, about 1 inch above your belly button.

04
Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, about 8 inches below your waist.

05
Inseam (bottoms)

Measure from the crotch seam to the bottom of the leg along the inner thigh.
