This Privacy Policy explains how EXPECTATIONS ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website at expectations.site.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Give Us

  • Name, email address, phone number when you create an account or place an order
  • Shipping address and billing information
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never store card details)
  • Communications with us via email or live chat
  • Photos you upload to our AI Virtual Try-On feature

Information Collected Automatically

  • Browser type, device type, operating system, IP address
  • Pages visited, time on site, referring URLs
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Cookie Policy)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • To provide customer support
  • To send marketing emails (only if you opt in)
  • To improve our website and AI Try-On features
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations

3. AI Try-On Photos

Photos you upload to our AI Virtual Try-On are sent to Anthropic's API for processing and are not stored on our servers. We do not use your photos to train AI models. Photos are processed in real-time and discarded immediately after the try-on is generated.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Our manufacturer — to fulfil your order (name, address, items ordered only)
  • DHL / FedEx — for shipping (name and address only)
  • Resend — email delivery (email address only)
  • Vercel / Railway — website hosting
  • Law enforcement when legally required

5. Your Rights (GDPR / POPIA)

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time for marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@expectations.site.

6. Data Retention

We retain order data for 7 years for tax and legal compliance. Marketing data is retained until you unsubscribe. You can request deletion of non-order data at any time.

7. Security

We use SSL encryption, Stripe's PCI-compliant payment processing, and industry-standard security practices. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us immediately.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit. They're essential for basic site functionality and help us improve your experience.

Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These are required for the website to work. They cannot be disabled.

  • Session cookie — keeps you logged in during your visit
  • Cart cookie — remembers items in your cart
  • Stripe cookies — required for secure payment processing

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it.

  • Google Analytics 4 — page views, session duration, traffic sources (anonymised)

Marketing Cookies

Used only if you consent. These allow us to show you relevant ads.

  • Meta Pixel — tracks conversions from Facebook/Instagram ads

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may break site functionality. To opt out of analytics cookies, visit Google Analytics Opt-out.
